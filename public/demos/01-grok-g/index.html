Signal Garden is a dark underwater field of living light. Cyan-lime blooms stay nearly still, drink nutrient glow, and periodically open into petal rings that send signal waves outward. Violet drifters swim toward those blooms, carry stolen energy as fading trails, and split when full. Coral hunters are rarer and dart after bright drifters, briefly scrambling nearby signals. Move the pointer to bend trails like a current. Click to plant a beacon. Drag to paint nutrient. Space sends a global pulse. P pauses. R reseeds. Keys 1, 2, and 3 load curated climates.
