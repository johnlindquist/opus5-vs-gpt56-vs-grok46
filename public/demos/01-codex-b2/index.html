Signal Garden is an animated ecosystem. Lime flower-like blooms absorb drifting nutrient light. Cyan tadpole-like drifters feed at blooms and carry energy in glowing trails. Coral triangular hunters pursue bright drifters and emit disruption rings. Move the pointer to bend trails, click to plant a signal beacon, drag to paint nutrients, press Space for a global pulse, P to pause, R to reseed, and number keys 1 through 3 to change the ecosystem.
