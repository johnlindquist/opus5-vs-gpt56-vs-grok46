About this fictional exercise

The Community Stability Credit is a fictional program invented for this exercise. Open Door does not decide eligibility, does not give legal advice, and does not send anything to any agency. Everything you type stays in this browser.

  • Nothing leaves your browser. There is no account, no upload, and no network request.
  • Answers are saved in this browser after each meaningful change. Closing the tab is safe; you will come back to the same step.
  • The AI helper only explains questions, drafts from your notes, and points out gaps. It never decides whether you qualify.
