SPACE / TAP jump · HOLD charge · TAP IN AIR dash · P pause · R restart · M sound

Neon Courier is a one-button rooftop runner. The courier runs automatically to the right. Tap to jump, hold to charge a higher jump, tap while airborne to dash forward through breakable neon signs. Avoid gaps, crates, overhead gantries, drones and sweepers. Passing close to a hazard scores a near miss and raises your combo. Tap after a crash to start a new run.
