Neon Courier

One-button rooftop heist. The courier runs on their own. Press Space, click, or tap to jump while grounded. Hold briefly on the ground to charge a higher jump. Tap in the air to dash if a dash is available. After a crash, tap to restart immediately. P pauses, R restarts, M toggles sound after the first action.
